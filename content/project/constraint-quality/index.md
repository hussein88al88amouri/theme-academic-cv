Which constraints actually help a clustering algorithm? This recurring question still lacks a sufficiently general and accurate answer.

My current research examines limitations in existing methods and develops ways to assess the benefit of a constraint. The aim is to reason more reliably about constraint quality, rather than assuming that every supplied constraint improves a clustering.
