I am an Assistant Professor and researcher at USAL in Lebanon. My research explores how limited supervision and expert knowledge can help us learn useful, interpretable representations of time-series data.

During my PhD at the University of Strasbourg, I developed Constrained DTW Preserving Shapelets (CDPS) and Shapelet Cluster Explanation (SCE). My current work asks which constraints are beneficial for clustering, and how their quality can be assessed reliably. I also collaborate with researchers at LAU on seizure detection using diffusion models and few-shot learning.

I welcome conversations about research collaborations, academic opportunities, and industry R&D.
